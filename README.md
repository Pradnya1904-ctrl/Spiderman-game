How to Play

    Start the game: Click the Left Mouse Button or press SPACE to throw a web and start.

    Movement: Use A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys to move Spider-Man horizontally.

    Shoot Web: Left Click anywhere on the screen to shoot a web towards that spot.

    Gwen Rescue (Priority): Gwen (Pink with yellow hair) falls down very frequently and fast. Target her with your web before she reaches the bottom, or the game ends immediately.

    Monsters & Civilians: Shoot webs at monsters (purple) to defeat them and at civilians (green) to rescue them for extra points.
